# A Randomized Trial of CardioRelief in Adults with Stable Heart Failure

## Abstract

Background: Stable heart failure remains associated with repeated hospitalization. Methods: We conducted a randomized, double-blind trial comparing CardioRelief plus standard care with placebo plus standard care. Results: The primary outcome, change in 6-minute walk distance at 24 weeks, improved by 38 meters in the CardioRelief group versus 14 meters in the placebo group.

## Methods

Eligible participants were 40 to 85 years of age, had stable heart failure for at least 3 months, left ventricular ejection fraction of 45% or lower, and a baseline 6-minute walk distance between 150 and 450 meters.

## Results

The adjusted between-group difference in the primary outcome was 24 meters (95% CI, 10 to 38; P=0.001). Serious adverse events occurred in 8.9% receiving CardioRelief and 10.6% receiving placebo.
